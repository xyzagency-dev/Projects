Clarity in Every Phase

Traditional projects create friction: the interior designer draws concepts, the general contractor complains about constructability, and the homeowner absorbs budget escalations. Atelier Aura operates as a unified Design-Build Atelier. Our architects, project managers, and licensed master craftsmen work under one roof, providing a guaranteed single-source contract and fixed timeline.

Following initial feasibility using our cost calculator, we prepare a detailed 100-page specification document with line-item transparency. Once the final contract is executed, we absorb scope variances that are not client-initiated change orders.

A typical 4,000–6,000 sq ft luxury gut renovation spans 6 to 10 months from permit issuance to key handover. Ground-up architectural estates typically range from 14 to 22 months depending on hillside engineering, concrete curing, and bespoke artisan millwork.

Every Atelier Aura residence includes a 15-year comprehensive structural warranty, a 3-year warranty on bespoke custom millwork and finishes, and dedicated white-glove concierge property maintenance for the first 24 months.
