# Atelier Aura — Luxury Interior Architecture & Turnkey Construction Platform

A turnkey, scalable and sellable digital platform for high-end interior architecture and construction studios.

---

## 🏛️ Features Included

1. **Turnkey Cost & Scope Estimator**: Interactive space typologies, dynamic sq. ft. slider, finish tiers, add-ons, and real-time itemized budget breakdown.
2. **Before & After Renovation Slider**: Interactive split comparison showing raw vs. completed architectural space.
3. **Style Archetype Finder**: 4-question interactive diagnostic with instant archetype recommendations.
4. **Tactile Material Studio**: Interactive inspection of marble, oak, champagne brass, and Belgian linen.
5. **Portfolio & Lightbox**: Filterable case studies with blueprints, specifications, and gallery viewer.
6. **Client Portal Simulation (Aura Track™)**: Real-time milestone tracker, daily site logs, and digital change-order approval.
7. **Consultation Scheduler**: Multi-step qualification booking modal with instant lookbook delivery.

---

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash
node server.js
```
Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
